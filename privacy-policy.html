This Privacy Policy explains how AI Nutrition Coach collects, uses, stores and protects information.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address and user ID.
  • Age, sex, height, weight, target weight, activity level and goals.
  • Dietary preferences, allergies, disliked foods and weekly budget.
  • AI requests, meal plans, history, favorites and shopping lists.
  • Technical data required for security and service operation.

How we use information

We use data to manage accounts, personalize meal plans, synchronize content, maintain security, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

The app uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and the Google Gemini API.

Retention and security

Data is retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. We use encrypted network connections and controlled database access.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information. See the Delete Account page.

Health disclaimer

The app provides informational meal-planning suggestions and does not replace advice from a physician, dietitian or qualified healthcare professional.
