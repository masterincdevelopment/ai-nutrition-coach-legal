This Privacy Policy explains how AI Nutrition Coach collects, uses, processes, stores and protects information when you use the app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and user ID.
  • Profile and nutrition information: age, sex, height, weight, target weight, dietary preferences, nutrition goals, disliked foods and weekly budget.
  • Health and physical activity information: information voluntarily provided by the user, such as allergies, intolerances, health-related considerations and activity level, when used to provide and personalize nutrition features.
  • Photos: when you choose to use the food scanning feature, a photo taken with your camera or selected from your gallery is securely transmitted to our backend and processed using the Google Gemini API to provide the requested food analysis. AI Nutrition Coach does not intentionally store the submitted photo in its application database or cloud storage. Processing by Google is subject to Google's applicable data processing and retention terms for the Gemini API.
  • App activity: information about interactions with the app and use of its features.
  • App diagnostics and performance: crash logs, diagnostic information and app performance data used to monitor reliability, diagnose technical problems and improve the service.
  • Device identifiers: device or installation identifiers and notification tokens required for app services such as push notifications and technical operation.
  • User content: AI requests, meal plans, history, favorites and shopping lists.

How we use information

We use information to create and manage user accounts, provide and personalize meal plans and nutrition features, analyze food photos when requested by the user, synchronize content, provide app functionality, deliver notifications, monitor app performance and crashes, maintain security, provide support and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Health, physical activity and nutrition-related information is used to provide and personalize the app experience and its nutrition and meal-planning features.

Health and physical activity information is not used by AI Nutrition Coach for advertising or marketing purposes.

AI-powered features

AI Nutrition Coach provides features powered by artificial intelligence, including personalized nutrition assistance and food photo analysis.

Information necessary to answer an AI request may be securely transmitted to our backend and processed using the Google Gemini API. Depending on the feature used, this information may include nutrition preferences, profile information, health-related considerations, physical activity information, meal information or a food photo voluntarily submitted by the user.

AI-generated information is provided for informational purposes and should not be considered medical advice.

Service providers

AI Nutrition Coach uses Google and Firebase services to operate, secure and monitor the app. These services include:

  • Firebase Authentication;
  • Cloud Firestore;
  • Cloud Functions;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging;
  • Firebase Analytics;
  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring; and
  • Google Gemini API.

These services may process information as necessary to provide, secure, monitor and improve the functionality of AI Nutrition Coach, subject to their applicable privacy, security and data processing terms.

When you choose to analyze a food photo or use another AI-powered feature, information necessary to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to our backend and processed using the Google Gemini API.

Photos and food scanning

Access to your camera or photo gallery is used only when you choose to use the food scanning feature.

A selected or captured image is transmitted for the purpose of analyzing the food shown in the image and returning nutrition-related information.

AI Nutrition Coach does not intentionally store submitted food photos in its application database or cloud storage after the requested processing. Google may process or retain API request data according to the Gemini API configuration and Google's applicable data processing and retention terms.

Analytics, diagnostics and performance

We use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring to understand app interactions, identify crashes and technical problems, monitor performance and improve the reliability of the app.

These services may process app interaction information, diagnostic data, crash information, performance information and device or installation identifiers as necessary to provide these functions.

Notifications

AI Nutrition Coach may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to provide push notifications and app-related communications. Device or installation identifiers and notification tokens may be processed as necessary to deliver these notifications.

Data retention and security

Account, profile and other stored app data is retained while your account is active or for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

Photos submitted through the food scanning feature are not intentionally stored by AI Nutrition Coach in its application database or cloud storage. They are transmitted for processing through the Google Gemini API. Google may process or retain API request data according to the Gemini API configuration and applicable Google data processing and retention terms.

We use encrypted network connections and controlled database access to help protect information during transmission and storage.

Your rights and account deletion

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

You can delete your account using the account deletion function available within AI Nutrition Coach. Account deletion removes the account and associated app data in accordance with the app's deletion process and applicable legal retention requirements.

Information about deleting your account and associated data is also available on our Delete Account page.

Health disclaimer

AI Nutrition Coach provides informational meal-planning and nutrition suggestions and does not replace advice, diagnosis or treatment from a physician, registered dietitian or other qualified healthcare professional.

Users should seek appropriate professional advice regarding medical conditions, allergies, dietary restrictions or other health concerns.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features or applicable legal requirements change. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the Privacy Policy was last updated.
