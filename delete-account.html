Delete your AI Nutrition Coach account and associated data

You can delete your account directly from the app or request account deletion by email.

Delete your account inside the app

AI Nutrition Coach provides an account deletion function directly inside the app.

  1. Open AI Nutrition Coach.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

For security reasons, you may be asked to sign in again before account deletion can be completed.

Request account deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by sending an email from the email address associated with your AI Nutrition Coach account.

Use the subject: Delete my AI Nutrition Coach account.

Request account deletion

We may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner before processing the deletion.

What will be deleted

When your account is deleted, the account and associated app data are deleted in accordance with the AI Nutrition Coach deletion process. This may include:

  • your authentication account;
  • your profile and account information;
  • nutrition preferences and goals;
  • health and physical activity information stored in your profile;
  • saved meal plans and related history;
  • favorites;
  • shopping lists and related app data; and
  • device notification information associated with your account, where applicable.

Food scanner photos

Photos voluntarily submitted through the food scanning feature are transmitted for processing when you request an analysis. AI Nutrition Coach does not intentionally store these photos in its application database or cloud storage.

Processing performed by external service providers is subject to their applicable data processing and retention terms, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Processing time

Deletion performed through the in-app deletion function is initiated when you confirm the request and successfully complete any required security verification.

Verified deletion requests submitted by email are generally processed within 30 days.

Limited information may be retained when necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security or prevent fraud and abuse.
